NovaMart Refund and Returns Policy
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 January 2026

1. Return window
Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days after delivery for eligible products.

2. Damaged or incorrect products
If a product arrives damaged or the customer receives the wrong item, the customer should contact NovaMart Support within 7 calendar days after delivery. The customer should provide the order number and clear photographs of the product and packaging. Support reviews the evidence and may approve a replacement or refund.

3. Refund processing
After an approved return is received and inspected, NovaMart initiates the refund to the original payment method. Banks and payment providers may take 5 to 10 business days to display the refund.

4. Non-returnable items
Personalized products and items marked final sale are not eligible for return unless they arrive damaged or incorrect.

5. Missing information
This policy does not specify employee relocation benefits or a guaranteed refund timeline before a return is approved.
