NovaMart Shipping and Delivery Policy
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 January 2026

1. Delivery estimates
Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 7 business days after dispatch. These are estimates, not guarantees.

2. Tracking
Customers can use the tracking link in their shipping confirmation email to view shipment progress.

3. Delayed orders
If tracking has not changed for 3 business days or the estimated delivery window has passed, the customer should contact NovaMart Support. Support checks the carrier status and provides the next steps.

4. Lost packages
If the carrier confirms a package is lost, Support escalates the case to the Shipping Operations team for investigation and resolution.

5. Missing information
This policy does not define compensation amounts for delayed shipments.
