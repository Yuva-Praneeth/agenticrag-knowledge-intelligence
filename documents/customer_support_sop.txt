NovaMart Customer Support Standard Operating Procedure
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 January 2026

1. Initial response
Support agents should acknowledge a new customer request within one business day.

2. Required details
Before investigating an order issue, ask for the order number and a short description of the problem. Request photographs when the claim concerns damage or an incorrect item.

3. Escalation
Escalate suspected payment fraud, repeated delivery failures, safety concerns, and cases requiring an exception to a policy to the relevant specialist team.

4. Evidence and communication
Agents should summarize the steps taken, record relevant evidence in the case, and explain the next step to the customer without promising an outcome that has not been approved.

5. Unsupported questions
If approved documentation does not answer a question, do not guess. Explain that the policy documents do not contain the answer and refer the case to a supervisor when necessary.
